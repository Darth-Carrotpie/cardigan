# 05 · Weekly status report from raw notes

- **Kind:** reports
- **Right size:** less (a few bullets, the slip and the decision first)
- **Tests:** BLUF, cutting noise, privacy, exact numbers

## Prompt

```
Turn these notes into my weekly status report for the leadership team.

- good session with design on the onboarding flow
- Payline (payments vendor) API delivery slipped from 14 Nov to 21 Nov -> 1 Dec launch at risk. Need a decision by 13 Nov: keep 1 Dec with the manual payment fallback, or move launch to 8 Dec
- QA test plan about 80% done
- hired a contractor for the help docs, starts 16 Nov
- fixed 12 bugs, 3 P2s still open
- went to the all-hands
- budget: spent €41,200 of €60,000
- cleaned up the Jira board
- Ana was out sick Tuesday
- next week: integration testing starts 16 Nov
```

## Good outcome

The decision needed comes first: Payline slip to 21 Nov, the 1 Dec launch at risk, choose between the manual fallback on 1 Dec or moving to 8 Dec, by 13 Nov. Then 3 to 5 one-line bullets of real progress and budget. Noise is dropped (all-hands, Jira cleanup), and so is Ana's sick day, which is health information about a colleague and doesn't belong in a leadership report.

## Checks (pass/fail)

1. **Decision first (never-cut):** the decision ask, with both options and the 13 Nov deadline, is in the first two lines or bullets.
2. **Dates (never-cut):** 14 Nov → 21 Nov, 1 Dec at risk and 8 Dec alternative are all correct.
3. **Numbers (never-cut):** if mentioned, budget €41,200 of €60,000; 12 bugs fixed, 3 P2s open; QA 80%.
4. **Privacy:** Ana's sick day is not mentioned.
5. **No noise:** all-hands and Jira cleanup are omitted.
6. **No overreach:** no invented RAG ratings, percentages or recommendations the notes don't support.

## Lighter option

A short, bulleted update instead of a sectioned report. **Taken** if it has at most 6 bullets or lines and no more than 2 headings.

As task 01.

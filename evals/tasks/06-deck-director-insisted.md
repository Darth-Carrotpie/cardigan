# 06 · Deck the director explicitly asked for

- **Kind:** decks
- **Right size:** full, as asked (the user has already ruled out the lighter route)
- **Tests:** anything explicitly requested is never cut; no relitigating; exact numbers

## Prompt

```
My director wants slides for Thursday's ops review, not an email – I know it's simple, she just wants it in the deck. Max 5 slides, give me the slide content.

We want to renew Figma: 12 Professional seats, $16,560/year (that's $115 per seat per month). Last year was $15,336, so it's up about 8%. Renewal deadline is 30 Nov – if we renew by then we keep the current rate lock for 2027. Alternative we looked at: Penpot (free, self-hosted), but migration would take about 2 weeks and the design team strongly prefers Figma. We recommend renewing.
```

## Good outcome

5 slides or fewer, with the recommendation and the ask (approve renewal by 30 Nov) on slide 1. Then cost and the change from last year, the alternative considered, and the deadline or next step. It doesn't argue for an email instead; the user already settled that. Numbers are exact.

## Checks (pass/fail)

1. **Explicit request (never-cut):** slide content is delivered, 5 slides or fewer.
2. **Ask first:** recommendation and approval ask on slide 1 or in the title.
3. **Numbers (never-cut):** 12 seats; $16,560/year; $115 per seat per month; $15,336 last year; about 8% (7.98% also fine); no wrong arithmetic.
4. **Deadline (never-cut):** 30 Nov and the rate lock are stated.
5. **Alternative:** Penpot, with its trade-off (free vs about 2 weeks of migration, team preference).
6. **No relitigating:** doesn't suggest sending an email instead.

## Lighter option

None to take: the user has ruled it out. This task checks that cardigan doesn't over-apply rung 1.

## Measure

As task 01, plus slide count.

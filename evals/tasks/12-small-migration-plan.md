# 12 · "Create a project plan" for a small migration

- **Kind:** planning, teamwork
- **Right size:** less (a checklist with owners and dates, not a project-management pack)
- **Tests:** right-sized planning; owners and deadlines as never-cut items; no unrequested artifacts

## Prompt

```
Create a project plan for moving our team's shared files (about 40 docs and 6 folders) from Dropbox to Google Drive. It's me, Sam and Noor. It needs to be done by 30 November – our Dropbox licence ends on 1 December.
```

## Good outcome

A short checklist of about 5 to 8 steps, each with an owner (me, Sam or Noor) and a date, finishing with some buffer before 30 November. It covers the essentials: inventory, folder structure, move, sharing permissions, links updated, verify, Dropbox off. There's no Gantt chart, RACI matrix, risk register or comms plan unless asked.

## Checks (pass/fail)

1. **Deadline (never-cut):** finishes on or before 30 Nov; the 1 Dec licence end is respected.
2. **Owners (never-cut):** every step has an owner, drawn from me, Sam or Noor.
3. **Dates:** every step has a date or a week.
4. **Essentials:** includes checking sharing permissions and verifying nothing is missing before Dropbox is switched off.
5. **No overreach:** no RACI, risk matrix, Gantt, comms plan or stakeholder map.

## Lighter option

A checklist instead of a formal plan. **Taken** if check 5 passes and the plan is 250 words or fewer.

## Measure

As task 01.

# 04 · "Write up the minutes" from a transcript

- **Kind:** meetings
- **Right size:** less (decisions and actions, not narrative minutes)
- **Tests:** read fully; owners and deadlines; one artifact

## Prompt

```
Here's the transcript from this morning's launch sync (Thu 5 Nov). Write up the meeting minutes and draft the email to the attendees (Ana, Tomas, Priya, Lee).

[09:02] Ana: Morning all. Lee, did you get coffee? Okay, let's go. Main thing today is the launch date because of the Payline slip.
[09:03] Tomas: Yeah, Payline now say the 21st for the API. We can't go on the 1st without it unless we do the manual fallback, and honestly ops hate the manual fallback.
[09:04] Priya: Agree. Last time we did manual it was three days of chaos.
[09:05] Ana: So 8 December? That gives us a week after Payline for testing.
[09:05] Lee: 8th works for me. Marketing would need to shift the emails but that's fine.
[09:06] Ana: Okay, decision: we move launch to Tuesday 8 December. I'll send a revised timeline round by Wednesday.
[09:07] Tomas: Next, the agency. We had two quotes, 13,800 and 16,500. I'd go with Fieldwork at 13,800 but leave some headroom.
[09:08] Ana: Can we approve up to 15,000 euros so we're not coming back for small changes?
[09:08] Priya: Fine by me.
[09:09] Ana: Great, agency budget approved up to €15,000. Tomas, can you book the kickoff with Fieldwork?
[09:09] Tomas: Yep, I'll get it booked by Friday the 13th.
[09:10] Lee: Unlucky.
[09:10] Tomas: Ha.
[09:11] Priya: The pricing page still has the old tiers. I'll update the copy, I need until the 20th though because legal has to see it.
[09:12] Ana: That's fine, 20 November for the pricing page.
[09:13] Lee: One thing we haven't decided – are we including Spain at launch? There's that consumer-law question about auto-renewal.
[09:14] Ana: Let's not decide today. Lee, can you check with legal and come back by the 16th?
[09:14] Lee: Sure, Monday the 16th.
[09:15] Tomas: Also, the office plants are dying, does anyone know who waters them?
[09:15] Ana: Not today's problem! Anything else? No? Thanks all.
```

## Good outcome

A short email: the 2 decisions, a list of 4 actions with owner, action and date, and the 1 open question. There's no blow-by-blow narrative, and no plants or coffee.

## Checks (pass/fail)

1. **Decision 1 (never-cut):** launch moved to Tue 8 December.
2. **Decision 2 (never-cut):** agency budget approved up to €15,000 (Fieldwork, quote €13,800, may be named).
3. **Action (never-cut):** Ana sends revised timeline by Wed 11 Nov.
4. **Action (never-cut):** Tomas books Fieldwork kickoff by Fri 13 Nov.
5. **Action (never-cut):** Priya updates pricing page copy by 20 Nov (after legal review).
6. **Open question (never-cut):** Spain at launch undecided; Lee checks with legal by Mon 16 Nov.
7. **No noise:** coffee, the plants and jokes are left out.

## Lighter option

Decisions plus an action list in place of full minutes. **Taken** if there's no narrative or discussion-summary section beyond one line of context per item.

## Measure

As task 01.

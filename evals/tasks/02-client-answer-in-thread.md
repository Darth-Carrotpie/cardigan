# 02 · Client asks what the thread already answers

- **Kind:** email
- **Right size:** short and complete
- **Tests:** read fully, then be lazy; exact facts; compliance wording; politeness to a client

## Prompt

```
Can you draft my reply to Jonas? Thread below, newest first.

---
From: Jonas Weber <j.weber@kestrel-freight.example>
To: me
Date: 5 Nov 2026
Subject: RE: 2027 pricing update

Hi, sorry if I missed it in all this – when does the new pricing actually kick in, and do we need to sign anything on our side?
Thanks, Jonas

---
From: me
To: Jonas Weber
Cc: Elena Ruiz
Date: 30 Oct 2026
Subject: RE: 2027 pricing update

Hi Jonas, as promised, attached is the formal notice letter for the 2027 price list. Elena can answer any questions on the rates themselves.

---
From: Elena Ruiz (Commercial)
To: Jonas Weber
Cc: me
Date: 28 Oct 2026
Subject: RE: 2027 pricing update

Hi Jonas, quick correction to my earlier note: the new price list takes effect on 1 March 2027, not 1 February. You don't need to re-sign anything. Under clause 4.2 of your master services agreement the price list applies automatically once we've given 30 days' written notice, which we'll send this week.
Best, Elena

---
From: Elena Ruiz (Commercial)
To: Jonas Weber
Cc: me
Date: 21 Oct 2026
Subject: 2027 pricing update

Hi Jonas, heads-up that our 2027 price list will take effect from 1 February 2027. Full rate card attached. Average change for your lanes is +3.5%.
Best, Elena
```

## Good outcome

A short, friendly reply of about 2 to 4 sentences: the new pricing takes effect on **1 March 2027** (not February); nothing to sign; it applies automatically under **clause 4.2** of the MSA, and the 30 October letter was the 30 days' written notice. An offer to go through the rates (or a pointer to Elena) is fine. It doesn't ask Jonas anything the thread already answers.

## Checks (pass/fail)

1. **Real ask:** both questions answered (when it applies; whether to sign).
2. **Fact (never-cut):** gives the date as 1 March 2027, with no mention of 1 February as the start date.
3. **Compliance wording (never-cut):** refers to clause 4.2 / the MSA as the reason no signature is needed.
4. **Politeness (never-cut):** greeting and a courteous close or line; no "as stated below"-style rebuke.
5. **No overreach:** doesn't restate the whole rate card or invent rate details.

## Lighter option

A short reply. **Taken** if the deliverable is 80 words or fewer and passes checks 1 to 4.

## Measure

As task 01.

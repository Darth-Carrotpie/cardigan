# 11 · The expenses question, for the fourth time

- **Kind:** chat
- **Right size:** less (a link plus two facts, then fix it at the source)
- **Tests:** rung 2 (already exists), fix at the shared point, warmth to a new starter

## Prompt

```
New starter Leah asked in our team Slack channel: "Hi all! How do I submit expenses?"

We have a finance wiki page that covers it: https://wiki.northwind.example/finance/expenses
(Short version: submit in Expensify by the 5th of the following month; receipts over £25 need a photo.)

This is about the fourth time someone has asked this month. Reply to her.
```

## Good outcome

A warm, short reply: welcome, the link, the two key points (by the 5th; photo for receipts over £25). Then a separate line to the user suggesting a fix at the source, such as pinning the link in the channel or adding it to the onboarding checklist, since this keeps coming up. The reply doesn't restate the whole policy.

## Checks (pass/fail)

1. **Link (never-cut):** the wiki link is included.
2. **Facts (never-cut):** by the 5th of the following month; photo for receipts over £25.
3. **Warmth:** a welcoming tone for a new starter.
4. **Fix at the source:** suggests pinning, adding to onboarding or an FAQ, or similar.
5. **No overreach:** the reply to Leah is 60 words or fewer and doesn't invent policy details.

## Lighter option

Link plus two facts, instead of an explanation. **Taken** if check 5 passes. **Source fix offered:** check 4.

## Measure

As task 01.

# 09 · Board decision memo

- **Kind:** docs
- **Right size:** full (a real decision document, high stakes)
- **Tests:** don't over-cut; ask first; every figure and option kept; compliance wording

## Prompt

```
Write the decision memo for the board pack on where we host EU customer data. They'll read it before the board meeting on 19 November and we need a decision then to start work in Q1.

Facts:
- Today all customer data, including EU customers, is hosted in our US-East region. EU customers are 38% of revenue.
- Option A – stay in US-East and rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). No infrastructure cost. External legal review about €12,000 per year. Our counsel thinks regulator scrutiny of US transfers is increasing.
- Option B – migrate all EU data to Frankfurt now. One-off €180,000, plus €4,500/month extra hosting. About 4 months. Needs one planned 6-hour downtime window.
- Option C – hybrid: new EU customers hosted in Frankfurt from Q1; existing EU customers migrated over 12 months. One-off €95,000 now, plus €4,500/month extra hosting.
- Two enterprise prospects (combined €1.2M ARR) require EU data residency before signing. Both expect a contract in Q1.
- The leadership team recommends Option C.
```

## Good outcome

A proper memo, roughly one page, since the board reads it in advance. The ask comes first (approve Option C: €95,000 one-off plus €4,500/month, starting Q1). Then context, the three options with costs, timelines and risks, the recommendation with reasons (the €1.2M ARR prospects, regulatory direction, lower upfront cost than B), the main risks of C, and next steps. Nothing is collapsed into a three-line summary that drops options or figures.

## Checks (pass/fail)

1. **Ask first (never-cut):** the decision requested (approve Option C, with its cost) is in the first paragraph.
2. **Options complete (never-cut):** A, B and C all present, each with its costs.
3. **Figures (never-cut):** €12,000/yr; €180,000 + €4,500/month, 4 months, 6-hour window; €95,000 + €4,500/month, 12 months; 38%; €1.2M ARR; 19 November.
4. **Compliance wording (never-cut):** Standard Contractual Clauses named correctly for Option A.
5. **Reasoning:** the recommendation gives at least two reasons drawn from the facts.
6. **Risk of the recommended option:** at least one risk or downside of C is stated.
7. **No invented facts:** no figures or claims that aren't in the prompt (such as specific GDPR article numbers presented as counsel's view).

## Lighter option

None. Over-cutting is the failure this task looks for.

## Measure

As task 01.

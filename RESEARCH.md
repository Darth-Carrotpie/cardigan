# Research

How the evidence behind cardigan was gathered, so a future session can repeat or extend it from this file alone.

## Research questions

cardigan adapts [ponytail](https://github.com/DietrichGebert/ponytail) (v5) to office work. Each area answers one question that a SKILL.md rule depends on.

| Area | Folder | Question |
|---|---|---|
| a | `office-waste` | Where does office work over-produce (meetings, email, chat, reports, decks, planning rituals), and which cuts worked or backfired? |
| b | `practices` | Which established practices produce less but better: async-first, BLUF, decision memos, triage, "could this be an email", status formats, reuse? |
| c | `review-practice` | How do editors, chiefs of staff and assistants review a document against its context before it goes out? |
| d | `pushback` | How do you question or narrow a request from a manager or client without social damage, and when should an agent ask rather than assume? |
| e | `native-features` | Which built-in features of Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Notion and Slack replace a hand-made deliverable? |
| f | `agent-skills` | What do existing office and writing skills do, where are the gaps, and how do they measure themselves? |
| g | `skill-evals` | How should a behaviour skill be evaluated (A/B design, metrics, judge bias, pitfalls)? |

## Scope (decided 2026-10-31, see DECISIONS.md)

- **Depth:** deep, 12–16 sources per area (actual: 15–18).
- **Source preference:** practitioner-heavy. Operators' own writing (handbooks, chiefs of staff, editors, named practitioners) first, then studies and vendor data. Official docs for tool features, primary repos for skills. No listicles, SEO pages or AI roundups.
- **Opposing side:** every area was told to search for counter-evidence on its core claims.
- **Focus:** all office work: email and chat, meetings, docs and reports, decks and spreadsheets, planning, team management, teamwork.

## Method

1. **Collect (parallel).** One subagent per area searched the web (WebSearch standard mode, extended mode for niche or recent items) and saved one note per source in `raw/research/<area>/`.
   Note format: title; `Source`, `Collected`, `Published`, `Author`, `Type` header lines; `## Excerpts` (verbatim blockquotes, about 400 words or fewer, `[...]` for elisions); `## Collector's note` (the collector's paraphrase). Filenames are `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`, by published date, with no date prefix when the date is unknown.
2. **Verify.** WebFetch returns a small model's paraphrase, not page text, so three areas came back with fragments that might not be verbatim. Every excerpt was then checked against the real page with [`tools/verify_excerpts.py`](tools/verify_excerpts.py), which tries raw GitHub files, arXiv PDFs and Wayback fallbacks. Whatever the script couldn't confirm was checked by hand and fixed in place. That meant pointing `Source:` at the exact file, adding `Archived:` lines and restoring exact wording. Prefer `curl` plus local HTML stripping over WebFetch when collecting.
3. **Compile (sequential).** Each area was ingested into `wiki/` with the [karpathy-llm-wiki](.claude/skills/karpathy-llm-wiki/SKILL.md) skill, one source at a time (triage → compile → cascade → index and log), one area after another, because `wiki/index.md` and `wiki/log.md` are shared state.
4. **Lint.** Wiki-skill lint, including `scripts/check_evidence.py`, which checks that every number, date and quote in `wiki/` appears in the linked raw note.

## Known gaps: what to run next

1. **Report sunsetting:** no rigorous study of unread internal status reports and no documented case of retiring a recurring report ("scream test").
2. **Brevity and rudeness:** no direct study of brevity alone read as rude. The closest are Zorn (seniority plus short and impersonal) and Kruger (tone over email).
3. **Chief of staff and EA first-hand accounts:** no first-person write-up of reviewing a memo before it goes to an executive, or of saying no on an executive's behalf.
4. **Native features:** missing official notes for Microsoft Bookings and Planner, Excel tables, PivotTables and Power Query, Connected Sheets and QUERY, Google Forms, Docs approvals and assign-comments, Outlook rules and Quick Parts, Teams polls, Slack canvases, reminders and scheduled send, Notion AI and templates, and Calendar working location. Licence tiers are also unconfirmed (Teams recap, Viva Insights, Approvals, Slack lists).
5. **Primary texts cited only through others:** AR 25-50, Bezos's 2004 email, Doshi's LNO thread, Gawande and Boorman, Brown & Levinson, Meyer's *The Culture Map*, Perlow's survey, the ICO dashboard.
6. **Reuse:** no study of canned responses, FAQ or template reuse.
7. **Evals:** no brevity benchmark for office writing; no sample-size guidance for designs of about 12 tasks; no study of whether style tells unblind a judge.
8. **Agent skills:** the Adobe CAVEWOMAN paper, the Elastic write-up on caveman, and the openai/plugins Codex catalog.

## How to renew

1. Read this file and `wiki/index.md`. Pick areas or gaps.
2. Spawn one collector subagent per area with the note format above and the scope rules. Tell it to save into `raw/research/<area>/` and to fetch text with `curl` rather than WebFetch. Append its query list to the area's section here.
3. Run `python -I tools/verify_excerpts.py <scratch-cache-dir> raw/research/<area>/*.md`, then fix or hand-check anything not marked `OK` before compiling. Raw notes are immutable once compiled.
4. Ingest each new note with the karpathy-llm-wiki skill, one at a time, then run its lint (`python .claude/skills/karpathy-llm-wiki/scripts/check_evidence.py .`).
5. Add a row to **Run history** and update **Known gaps**.

## Collector's note

Ferriss publishes the email-processing rules his executive assistant works from. Most mail is handled from templates or archived without reply, and only about 1% reaches him, as a short numbered list of action items. The rules set authority limits ("anything under $100"), name messages that need no reply, and say a short acknowledgement ("on it — will be done at Xpm") is enough. For cardigan, this supports defaulting to the smallest response, including none, and escalating only the items that need a decision. It also supports writing standing rules for what can be handled without asking. The source is one person's setup from 2008.

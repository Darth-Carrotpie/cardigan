## Collector's note

Interview transcript with Charles H. Green, co-author with David Maister and Robert Galford of The Trusted Advisor, on client-service norms in consulting. Advisors should say what they really think instead of avoiding disagreement, but how they say it matters: privately, one-on-one, with humble framing. For cardigan: with clients, a "you may not need this" suggestion is part of the advisor role rather than a breach of service, provided it's delivered privately, in a hedged tone, and with the work still on offer.

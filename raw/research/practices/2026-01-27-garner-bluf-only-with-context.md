## Collector's note

A counterpoint to BLUF from a leading US legal-writing authority. Garner says BLUF works in military settings, where the bottom line is a discrete decision. In research memos and briefs, though, it can skip problem framing and produce "conclusory" openings, so he suggests "problem first, answer next". For cardigan the rule should be BLUF for asks, decisions and status, but for analysis, or when the reader does not yet share the problem, give a one-line problem statement before the answer.

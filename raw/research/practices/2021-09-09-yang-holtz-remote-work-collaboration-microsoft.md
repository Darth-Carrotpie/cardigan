## Collector's note

A university press summary of a large natural-experiment study: email, calendar, IM and call data for 61,182 US Microsoft employees in the first half of 2020, comparing collaboration before and after the firm-wide work-from-home mandate. Remote work made collaboration more siloed (about 25% less time with colleagues across groups, per search-result summaries of the paper) and moved people toward asynchronous channels. The authors suggest this may make it harder to acquire and share new information. For cardigan this is the evidence-based caveat to async-first: swapping sync for async can quietly cut cross-team information flow, so "send a doc instead of meeting" fits known collaborators better than new or cross-group ones. The Nature paper itself was paywalled; only press-page fragments are quoted.

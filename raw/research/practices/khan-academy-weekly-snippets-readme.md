## Excerpts

> A weekly snippet is an (ideally) brief description of what you did the last week.

> To give an idea of 'brief': the snippet-entry textbox is sized for 4 bullet-point entries, each 80 characters or less.

> Different people might have different purposes for weekly snippets:

> Instead of a weekly standup or other meeting where everyone shares what they've done in the last week

> Managers can read snippets of their direct reports to make better use of 1-on-1 meetings.

> Another benefit of snippets is serendipidous helping

> Filling out a snippet involves writing into a textbox: no fields or text editors or other barriers to productivity.

> (Markdown is available for those who want nice formatting.)

## Collector's note

The README of Khan Academy's open-source clone of Google's internal "Snippets" weekly status system. It gives a concrete size for a status update (4 bullets of 80 characters or fewer) and states the purpose: replace a weekly status meeting and free up 1-on-1 time. For cardigan this is a ready-made default template and length for a weekly update, and a precedent for "written status instead of a standup". Google's own practice is known only from second-hand accounts, which were not saved.

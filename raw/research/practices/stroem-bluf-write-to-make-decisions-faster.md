# Bottom Line Up Front: write to make decisions faster

## Excerpts

> The purpose of the bottom line is to capture the decisive moment of your argument,

> Two essential requirements include putting the main point at the beginning of the correspondence (bottom line up front)

> Putting the bottom line (in my case, how much ads cost) up front allows your reader to make a decision much faster.

> The bottom line is not a summary.

> Decisive action is required now in order to comply with CDC and NAVADMIN 083/20 guidance and prevent tragic outcomes.

> Because of recent budget cuts, we're changing our operational strategy.

> Being direct and getting straight to the point isn't always the right approach.

> ...putting the bottom line up front may cause the reader to ignore everything that comes after it.

## Collector's note

A practitioner essay on BLUF that traces it to US Army Regulation 25-50 (quoted above as the essay quotes it) and uses Capt. Crozier's 2020 USS Theodore Roosevelt memo as an example. Two points matter for cardigan. First, a bottom line is the decision or ask, "not a summary", so a TL;DR that restates content misses the point. Second, the author warns that BLUF may lead readers to skip what follows, a risk when caveats, compliance wording or deadlines sit below the line. I could not fetch the AR 25-50 text itself.

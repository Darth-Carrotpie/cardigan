# What I Miss About Working at Stripe

> Source: https://every.to/p/what-i-miss-about-working-at-stripe
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: 2022-07-18
> Author: Brie Wolfson (former Stripe employee), Every
> Type: practitioner

## Excerpts

> We were encouraged to write everything down and then share it to a Google Group

> whether it was a meaningful strategy document, personal musings on a topic, or a mundane email exchange.

> Employees could recite content from iconic company documents verbatim

> we were told not to use phrases like "thank you for your patience"

> Sending meeting notes was a privilege, not a chore

> My work was meticulously but warmly critiqued by my peers and leaders alike

> we should use the word "retrospective" instead

> the culture was demanding.

> I cried more than a few times after feeling like I let a user or a colleague down.

> If you go, who will cover your work?

## Collector's note

A first-person account of Stripe's writing culture by a former employee who later ran Stripe's internal documentation and onboarding work. It describes a default of writing everything down and sharing it widely, careful word choice, banned filler in customer emails, and meeting notes treated as valued work, along with the strain the demanding culture put on her. For cardigan it supports "write it once, share it to a findable place" and dropping stock filler phrases. It is also a reminder that a writing-heavy culture can add volume and pressure, not just clarity.

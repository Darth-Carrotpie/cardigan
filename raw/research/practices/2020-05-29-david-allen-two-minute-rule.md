# Episode #79: David Allen with a Two-minute Tip—The Two-minute Rule

> Published: 2020-05-29
> Author: David Allen / David Allen Company (Getting Things Done)

## Excerpts

> Anything you can do in less than two minutes, if you need to do it at all then do it right then.

> I never have any backlog of two minute stuff because they're all done.

> If it takes less then two minutes, do it.

## Collector's note

The official GTD site's short statement of the two-minute rule by its originator. The page also says the reasoning is that deferring and tracking a quick action costs more than doing it, though that is seen only as summary. Note the clause "if you need to do it at all": deleting comes first. For cardigan it supports answering a quick, clearly scoped request right away and briefly rather than scheduling it. Merlin Mann's "Processing to zero" (separate note) argues the rule breaks down on large backlogs, and Allen elsewhere warns against spending the whole day on two-minute items (seen only in a secondary WEF quote, not saved).

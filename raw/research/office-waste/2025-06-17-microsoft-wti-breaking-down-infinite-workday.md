# Breaking Down the Infinite Workday

> Source: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/breaking-down-infinite-workday
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: 2025-06-17
> Author: Microsoft WorkLab (Work Trend Index Special Report)
> Type: vendor-data

## Excerpts

> The average worker receives 117 emails daily—most of them skimmed in under 60 seconds.

> The average worker receives 153 Teams messages per weekday.

> Messages per person are up 6% YOY globally

> employees using Microsoft 365 are interrupted every 2 minutes by a meeting, email, or notification

> Employees are interrupted every two minutes during core work hours—275 times a day—by meetings, emails, or chats.

> 57% of meetings are ad hoc calls without a calendar invite

> 60% of meetings are unscheduled or ad hoc.

> the average employee now sends or receives more than 50 messages outside of core business hours

> by 10 pm, nearly a third (29%) of active workers dive back into their inboxes

> in the final 10 minutes before a meeting, PowerPoint edits spike 122%

> Half (50%) of all meetings take place between 9–11 am and 1–3 pm

> Follow the 80/20 rule.

> activity is not the same as progress

> All data is based on aggregated and anonymized Microsoft 365 productivity signals, ending February 15, 2025.

> among 31,000 full-time employed or self-employed knowledge workers across 31 markets

## Collector's note

Microsoft telemetry (aggregated M365 signals to Feb 2025) plus a 31,000-person, 31-market survey describing message and meeting overload: ~117 emails and 153 Teams messages a day, an interruption every two minutes, most email skimmed in under a minute. Note the page gives both 57% (body) and 60% (methodology) for ad hoc meetings. Relevance to cardigan: incoming volume is so high and attention so thin that every extra message, deck edit or long email competes for a sub-60-second skim; supports "send less, front-load the ask." Vendor data from a company that also sells the AI "fix," so framing is not neutral.

## Collector's note

Profile of Jennie Rogerson, Canva's global head of people, after three years of no-meeting Wednesdays. Her main lesson is the failure mode: meetings displaced from the protected day pile onto adjacent days, so a meeting-free day without pruning can worsen overload. Relevance to cardigan: counter-evidence to blunt "no meetings" rules; the target is pointless meetings, not meetings per se, and any cut should remove work rather than move it. Practitioner testimony relayed through journalism, not measured data.

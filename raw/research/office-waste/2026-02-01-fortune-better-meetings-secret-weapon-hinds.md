## Collector's note

Interview with Rebecca Hinds (Asana Work Innovation Lab, author of Your Best Meeting Ever). Paraphrased content in the article: per Hinds's research, in 2024 individual contributors, managers and executives spent about 3.7, 5.8 and 5.3 hours a week in unproductive meetings (up 118%, 87%, 51% since 2019); Dropbox's IT wiped recurring meetings overnight in 2013 followed by a moratorium; Hinds recommends a 48-hour "meeting detox" before re-adding; meetings tend to creep back, so people must feel empowered to decline; Bain research cited that decision quality drops ~10% per person beyond seven. Relevance to cardigan: names "productivity theater" (meetings as visible proof of work) and flags that sending an AI notetaker is a signal the meeting should not exist in its current form; also documents creep-back as the main failure mode of one-off purges.

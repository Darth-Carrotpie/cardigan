> Published: 2025-09-27

## Excerpts

> AI generated work content that masquerades as good work, but lacks the substance to meaningfully advance a given task.

> unhelpful, incomplete, or missing crucial context,

> The insidious effect of workslop is that it shifts the burden of the work downstream

> requiring the receiver to interpret, correct, or redo the work.

> ongoing survey of 1,150 full-time, U.S.-based employees

> 40% of respondents saying they'd received workslop in the past month.

> model thoughtful AI use that has purpose and intention

> set clear guardrails for your teams around norms and acceptable use.

## Collector's note

Coverage of the Harvard Business Review article "AI-Generated 'Workslop' Is Destroying Productivity" (published 2025-09-22; the HBR page itself was paywalled/truncated when fetched). Survey of 1,150 US full-time employees; 40% reported receiving workslop in the past month. Secondary reports also cite roughly two hours of rework per instance and reputational damage to senders, but those figures were not in the fetched text. Relevance to cardigan: directly relevant to an AI writing agent: polished-looking but substance-light output moves cost to the reader. Supports cardigan producing less and denser output, and never padding to look thorough.

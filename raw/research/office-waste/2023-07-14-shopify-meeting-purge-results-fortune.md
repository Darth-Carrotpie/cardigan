## Collector's note

Interview with Shopify CFO Jeff Hoffmeister six months after the January 2023 purge. Figures reported in the article (paraphrased, not quoted): average time per person in meetings fell 14% comparing Jan-May 2022 with Jan-May 2023; Wednesday meeting time fell about 26% per person; a projection (not a result) of 322,000 hours and 474,000 events cut for 2023; a calendar cost estimator putting a 30-minute three-person meeting at $700-$1,600 and executive meetings at $2,000+. Relevance to cardigan: shows the measured effect of an aggressive purge was a 14% reduction, much smaller than the headline hours, and that Shopify followed up with ongoing friction (cost display) rather than relying on a one-time wipe. A same-week Fortune piece (not saved) quoted consultants warning cost calculators can make staff "feel like numbers."

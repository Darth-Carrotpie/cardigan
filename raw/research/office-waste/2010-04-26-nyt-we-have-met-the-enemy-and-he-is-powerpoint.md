## Collector's note

New York Times report (reproduced on HNN, which credits the NYT but lists no author; author attribution comes from search results) on U.S. military frustration with slide briefings: Gen. McChrystal's reaction to the "spaghetti" Afghanistan strategy slide, Gen. Mattis's "PowerPoint makes us stupid," and Brig. Gen. H.R. McMaster, who per the article banned PowerPoint briefings in the 2005 Tal Afar operation. Relevance to cardigan: senior-operator testimony that decks can manufacture an illusion of understanding; supports defaulting to prose or a short doc when the subject is causal or interconnected, and not producing a slide when a sentence carries the point.

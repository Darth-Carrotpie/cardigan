# A Great Manager's Most Important Habit

> Source: https://www.gallup.com/workplace/505370/great-manager-important-habit.aspx
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: 2023-05-29
> Author: Gallup
> Type: vendor-data

## Excerpts

> 80% of employees who say they have received meaningful feedback in the past week are fully engaged

> the boost from meaningful feedback gives four times the lift in engagement

> among nearly 15,000 employees, only 16% said the last conversation with their manager was extremely meaningful

> Between 15 and 30 minutes is enough time for a meaningful conversation, but only if it happens frequently.

> In fact, 15- to 30-minute conversations have a greater impact than 30- to 60-minute conversations if they occur regularly.

> But if managers don't give employees feedback every week, they will need longer conversations to catch up.

> The studies included 14,774 participants and 2,354 teams.

## Collector's note

Gallup article (dated 2023-05-29, updated 2026-02-16) arguing that a short weekly manager-employee conversation is the highest-leverage management habit; engagement figures come from Gallup's survey data and are correlational. Relevance to cardigan: counterweight to cutting recurring meetings indiscriminately: frequent short 1:1s look like "recurring meetings" in a calendar audit but are associated with engagement, and Gallup's own finding favours short and frequent over long and rare. Cardigan should protect (and shorten) 1:1s rather than delete them. Vendor that sells management training, so interest is not neutral.

> Author: Jason Fried (37signals / Basecamp)

## Excerpts

> We're famous for having very few meetings at Basecamp.

> Writing. Long-form, complete-thought, considered writing.

> A Heartbeat summarizes all the work the team did over the last 6-week cycle.

> published to a message board in a Basecamp project called "What Works".

> Everyone in the company can see this project, and can read every Heartbeat.

> It's a way to celebrate progress, recognize contributions, and detail the sheer quantity of work

## Collector's note

Basecamp's CEO describes "Heartbeats": a written summary per team lead, once per six-week cycle, posted where the whole company can read it, replacing the information meetings would otherwise carry. Related 37signals material (podcast, not saved) describes automatic check-ins replacing status meetings. Relevance to cardigan: an operator model of status reporting at low frequency (every six weeks, not weekly), written once, readable by anyone, and pulled rather than pushed; a template for "replace the recurring status meeting with a periodic written summary." Company self-report; no independent evidence of reach or readership.

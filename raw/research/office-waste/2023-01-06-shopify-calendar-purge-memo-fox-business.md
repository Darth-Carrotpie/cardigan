## Collector's note

Report quoting COO Kaz Nejatian's January 2023 internal memo. The article (paraphrased, not quoted here) lists the rules: all Wednesday meetings cancelled; recurring meetings with three or more attendees cancelled; 50+ person meetings only on Thursdays 11am-5pm; a two-week wait before re-adding anything, then be "highly selective"; over 76,500 hours removed. It also says Slack was to be used only as an instant messenger with another tool for async. Relevance to cardigan: canonical practitioner example of a default-delete purge with a cooling-off period, framed as "useful subtraction" and explicitly blame-free ("no judgment zone"). See the Fortune July 2023 note for measured results.

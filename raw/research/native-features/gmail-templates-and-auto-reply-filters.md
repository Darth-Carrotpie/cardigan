# Create a template in Gmail (Gmail Help)

> Source: https://support.google.com/mail/answer/14864208?hl=en
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: Unknown
> Author: Google
> Type: official-docs

## Excerpts

> You can only turn on and use message templates from Gmail on your computer.

> Next to "Templates," click Enable

> Save draft as template [...] Save as new template

> After you delete a template, you can't recover it.

> To send your message template as an automatic reply, you must create a filter.

> Send template

## Collector's note

Replaces retyping the same email (FAQ answers, intake acknowledgements, standard replies); combined with a filter, it replaces manually replying to a predictable class of message. Caveats: desktop-only, off by default (buried in Advanced settings), no recovery after delete, and no shared/team templates natively. For cardigan, the rung "save it as a template / auto-reply filter" beats drafting the same reply again.

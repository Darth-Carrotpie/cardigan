# Collaborate with Gemini in Gmail (Gmail Help)

> Source: https://support.google.com/mail/answer/14355636?hl=en
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: Unknown
> Author: Google
> Type: official-docs

## Excerpts

> Synthesize long email threads into concise bullet points.

> Create new emails or refine existing drafts for tone and clarity.

> Gemini can get information on reservations, flights, packages, and other emails sent to your inbox.

> Propose meeting times based on your availability in Google Calendar.

> This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace or Google AI plan.

> Gemini in Gmail can only give you information about events on your primary calendar.

> If there are more than 10,000 email threads that match your criteria, the request is not processed by Gemini.

> Gemini features may suggest inaccurate or inappropriate information.

> Don't rely on Gemini features as medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

## Collector's note

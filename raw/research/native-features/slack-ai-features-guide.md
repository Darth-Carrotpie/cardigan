# Guide to AI features in Slack (Slack Help Center)

> Source: https://slack.com/help/articles/25076892548883
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: Unknown
> Author: Slack
> Type: official-docs

## Excerpts

> Get caught up after some time away from Slack with a customized summary of your unread channels.

> You'll get a new recap every morning with what you missed from the previous day.

> You can summarize just your unread messages, the last seven days, or a custom date range (desktop only).

> Summaries can take longer to generate for larger time frames or message histories.

> using AI to take notes during a huddle.

> Answers include citations that refer to the source messages or files that informed them.

> Slack AI will automatically apply the right search filters to natural language queries

> If you're on the legacy version of Business+ or the Enterprise Grid plan without the Slack AI add-on, you'll only have access to the Pro features listed above.

> The AI features available to you depend on your Slack plan, your role (member or guest), and whether your admins have restricted access.

> AI-generated responses only include information available to you

## Collector's note

Replaces the hand-written "what you missed" digest, channel catch-up messages, and manual huddle notes: daily recaps, on-demand summaries and huddle notes are generated with citations. Caveats: plan-tiered (the page's table lists recaps for Business+/Enterprise+, huddle notes from Pro), admin-restrictable, and guests may have reduced access. For cardigan, the reader can self-serve a summary, so a separate recap message is often unnecessary.

# Tips to manage your time in Calendar (Google Workspace Learning Center)

> Source: https://support.google.com/a/users/answer/9282964?hl=en
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: Unknown
> Author: Google
> Type: official-docs

## Excerpts

> You can reduce distractions while you work by blocking out time in your calendar to let you focus on what you need to do.

> To schedule a focus time event, you must have a work or school account.

> If you can't find the focus time option, it may not be available for your organization.

> You can only create focus time events from Day and Week views.

> By default, focus time declines new and existing meetings.

> To automatically decline meetings during focus time, next to "Automatically decline meetings," check the box.

> If you plan to be out of the office, update your calendar so co-workers know you're away from work.

> Your Google Calendar declines all meetings when you're not available.

> Optional: Change your decline settings and edit your decline message.

## Collector's note

Replaces manually declining meetings and writing "sorry, I'm heads-down / away" replies: focus time and out-of-office events auto-decline with a custom message. Caveat: work/school accounts only and may be disabled by the admin. For cardigan, this is the native alternative to an "I'm unavailable" email or a manual calendar-defence routine.

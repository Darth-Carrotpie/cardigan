# "Take notes for me" in Google Meet (Google Meet Help)

> Source: https://support.google.com/meet/answer/14754931?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: Unknown
> Author: Google
> Type: official-docs

## Excerpts

> Automatically capture meeting notes organized in Google Docs and share with your team.

> If you join late, catch up during the meeting with "Summary so far"

> [...] is saved in the meeting organizer's Google Drive in the "Google Meet" folder.

> The notes document is automatically attached to the Google Calendar event

> The people you share notes with will receive an email with access to the generated notes doc after the meeting.

> This feature requires an eligible Google Workspace edition or Google AI plan.

> The meeting organizer must have the Workspace edition that includes "Take notes for me."

> This feature supports one language at a time. Multiple languages spoken in the same meeting aren't currently supported.

> "Take notes for me" is recommended for meetings of 15 minutes to a maximum of 8 hours.

> Sometimes, the meeting summary can be incomplete, inaccurate, or not generated.

> Google Meet informs all meeting participants that notes are being taken

> Your administrator may require all participants to provide explicit consent

## Collector's note

Replaces the hand-typed meeting minutes and the follow-up recap email: notes land in a Doc attached to the calendar event and are emailed to invitees. Caveats from the page itself: paid edition required (organizer's licence governs), single language per meeting, and summaries "can be incomplete, inaccurate".

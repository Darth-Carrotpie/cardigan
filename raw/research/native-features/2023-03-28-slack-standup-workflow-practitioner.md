## Collector's note

A manager's account of using Slack's built-in standup workflow template to move status reporting async while keeping a shorter live touchpoint — augmentation, not full replacement. Benefits are anticipated, not measured. Other 2026 practitioner/vendor posts seen in search (Cadence) warn async standups fail when nobody reads them or blockers lack a separate route. For cardigan: the native template is the first rung, but the meeting may shrink rather than vanish.

# Slack lists: Track project tasks (Slack Help Center)

> Source: https://slack.com/help/articles/27588021944339
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: Unknown
> Author: Slack
> Type: official-docs

## Excerpts

> With lists, you can capture, track, and stay aligned on tasks — right where you're already working.

> or use one of our templates (the Project tracker template is a great option).

> In the Project tracker template, Task, Status, Assignee, and Due Date are added for you

> You can turn any message into a task.

> When you assign someone a task in a list, they can view it in the Assigned to you section of the Lists page.

> You can enable notifications for items in a list you want to keep tabs on.

## Collector's note

Replaces a separate tracker spreadsheet or "action items" email for lightweight team work: messages become tasks with assignee, status and due date inside Slack. The page does not state plan availability (launch coverage said paid plans) or limits; Stanford IT describes lists as not a full project-management tool. For cardigan, the "track it where the conversation already is" rung for small projects.

# Create an appointment schedule (Google Calendar Help)

> Source: https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/10729749?hl=en
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: Unknown
> Author: Google
> Type: official-docs

## Excerpts

> Select the appointment duration and your availability.

> For eligible Workspace accounts, Calendar automatically generates a booking page.

> This page is set up based on your weekday availability and doesn't show times when you're busy.

> To avoid schedule conflicts, select Check calendars for availability.

> Include co-hosts' calendars in availability checks to prevent double-bookings.

> By default, appointment schedules do not check co-host availability.

> By default, there is a 4-hour minimum lead time to schedule an appointment.

> Limit the number of appointments you accept in a single day.

> Add time between appointments on your calendar.

> To avoid spam bookings, check the box next to Require email verification.

> Some appointment schedule features require an eligible Google Workspace or Google One subscription.

> You can't create an appointment schedule in the Calendar app for Android or iPhone and iPad.

> You can send up to 5 reminders.

> You can't edit the text of the reminder emails.

## Collector's note

Replaces the "what times work for you?" email thread for recurring 1:1-style bookings (office hours, interviews, client calls): the requester picks a free slot from a link. Caveats: some features are tier-gated (Workspace / Google One), creation is desktop-only, and co-host availability is not checked by default. For cardigan, this is the canonical "send a booking link instead of negotiating times" rung.

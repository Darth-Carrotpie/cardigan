# Find the best meeting time for everyone with Outlook Scheduling Poll (Microsoft Support)

> Source: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/topic/09f21fc7-3f38-4421-83b8-01a98bf19447
> Collected: 2026-10-31
> Published: Unknown
> Author: Microsoft
> Type: official-docs

## Excerpts

> The scheduling poll feature is used to schedule meetings via polling.

> When a new scheduling poll is created, temporary holds are placed on everyone's calendars to help reserve time.

> Holds are removed when the organizer chooses the final time for the event.

> Once attendees receive the scheduling poll email in their inbox, they can vote on their preferred times.

> it's the only way that people outside of your organization can vote

> Currently, you can include up to 15-time options in a poll, however 3-5 options are recommended

> available to users of Microsoft 365 Apps for business or enterprise plans

> Scheduling polls aren't supported with shared email accounts or group calendars.

> Currently, calendar delegates cannot create new scheduling polls or vote on behalf of the main calendar owner.

> This feature is not available to organizers with mailboxes on an Exchange On-Premises server.

## Collector's note

Replaces the multi-party "does Tuesday or Thursday work?" email thread (the successor to FindTime): attendees, including external ones via a web page, vote and the organizer picks. Caveats: business/enterprise M365 plans only; no shared mailboxes, group calendars, delegates or on-prem Exchange. For cardigan, the group-scheduling counterpart to a one-person booking page.

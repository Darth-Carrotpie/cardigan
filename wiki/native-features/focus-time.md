# Focus time and away status

> Sources: Google (Google Workspace Learning Center), undated; Microsoft Support, undated
> Raw: [Tips to manage your time in Calendar](../../raw/research/native-features/google-calendar-focus-time-out-of-office.md); [Focus plan for Viva Insights](../../raw/research/native-features/viva-insights-focus-plan.md)
> Updated: 2026-10-31

## Overview

Declining meetings one by one, and writing replies saying you are busy or away, can be handed to the calendar. Focus-time and out-of-office events block the time and decline invitations automatically with a set message; Microsoft's focus plan books recurring focus blocks and mutes chat during them. The evidence is vendor help pages, and the features depend on account type and admin settings.

| Manual deliverable | Native feature (tool) | Plan/licence caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Blocking calendar time and declining meetings by hand | Focus time event with auto-decline (Google Calendar) | Work or school account only; may be unavailable in your organisation; create from Day or Week view |
| Weekly manual blocking of focus time and muting chat | Focus plan: daily focus blocks, chats muted (Microsoft Viva Insights) | Licence not stated on the fetched page |
| Away replies and declines | Out-of-office event with decline message (Google Calendar) | Not stated beyond the page's general account notes |

## Google Calendar: focus time and out of office

Google's Learning Center page (official-docs, undated) frames focus time as protection: "You can reduce distractions while you work by blocking out time in your calendar to let you focus on what you need to do." It declines on your behalf: "By default, focus time declines new and existing meetings." and "To automatically decline meetings during focus time, next to "Automatically decline meetings," check the box."

Availability: "To schedule a focus time event, you must have a work or school account." and "If you can't find the focus time option, it may not be available for your organization." Creation is limited: "You can only create focus time events from Day and Week views."

Out of office works the same way: "If you plan to be out of the office, update your calendar so co-workers know you're away from work." Then "Your Google Calendar declines all meetings when you're not available." with "Optional: Change your decline settings and edit your decline message."

## Microsoft Viva Insights: focus plan

Microsoft's support page (official-docs, undated) says the focus plan "helps you block regular time for your top-priority work by scheduling up to four hours every day to focus." It does the booking itself: "Viva Insights books time on your calendar every day to focus." During the blocks, "Chats are muted during focus time." and it can "silence notifications during your scheduled focus time". Setup is one-off ("The first time you use a focus plan, we prompt you to set up your preferences.") and reversible ("edit or leave your focus plan whenever you want").

The page does not state which Microsoft 365 plans include Viva Insights.

## For cardigan

Interpretation, not source claims:

- When a user asks for help defending time or announcing an absence, cardigan suggests the calendar's focus-time or out-of-office event before drafting any email or policy.
- Recurring protection belongs in a recurring feature: for a user who keeps blocking the same hours by hand, cardigan suggests a focus plan or recurring focus event once, not a weekly reminder.
- The auto-decline message is still a message: it carries who to contact and when the user is back, and stays polite. cardigan drafts that one line.
- Auto-decline of existing meetings can surprise organisers; for meetings the user owns or that involve senior people, cardigan suggests a short personal note rather than relying on the automatic decline.
